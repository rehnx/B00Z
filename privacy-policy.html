Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Booz Assistant collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use Booz Assistant and its integrations with third-party services, including LinkedIn and Telegram.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

By authorizing Booz Assistant to access a third-party service, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. What Booz Assistant Does

Booz Assistant is a personal automation assistant designed to help users create, manage, and publish content through connected services.

For LinkedIn, Booz may use authorized LinkedIn API access to perform actions requested by the user, including creating and publishing posts after the user has provided the required authorization and, where configured, explicit approval.

Booz operates according to the permissions and automation rules configured by the user.

2. Information We May Access

LinkedIn Information

When you authorize LinkedIn access, Booz may process information made available through LinkedIn's APIs and the permissions granted to the application, which may include:

  • LinkedIn member identifier
  • Basic profile information made available through the authorized API
  • OAuth authorization information
  • Content created for publication
  • Information required to publish authorized content

Booz only requests and uses permissions necessary for the enabled functionality.

Telegram Information

If Telegram integration is enabled, Booz may process:

  • Telegram user or chat identifiers
  • Messages sent to the Booz bot
  • Commands and instructions provided to the bot
  • Content generated or requested through the bot
  • Information required to execute requested automation

Technical Information

Booz may process limited technical information required to operate and secure the service, including:

  • IP addresses
  • Request timestamps
  • Application logs
  • Error and diagnostic information
  • Authentication and authorization events

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Booz may be used to:

  • Authenticate connected services
  • Execute automation requested by the user
  • Generate and prepare content
  • Publish approved content
  • Respond to commands and instructions
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the application
  • Detect errors and unauthorized access
  • Maintain application security and reliability

Information obtained through LinkedIn authorization is used only for purposes related to the functionality authorized by the user.

4. LinkedIn API Data

Booz uses LinkedIn's official APIs to provide LinkedIn functionality.

LinkedIn information is processed only to provide functionality authorized by the user.

Booz does not sell LinkedIn member data.

Booz does not use LinkedIn data for advertising purposes.

Booz does not transfer LinkedIn data to unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Users may revoke LinkedIn authorization through the appropriate LinkedIn account settings.

5. Storage and Security

Booz may store authentication credentials, including OAuth access tokens, on the server used to operate the application.

Authentication credentials are intended to be stored securely and used only for authorized functionality.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested functionality, maintain security, resolve technical issues, or satisfy applicable obligations.

Information that is no longer required may be deleted from active application storage, subject to technical limitations and legitimate retention requirements.

7. Data Sharing

Booz does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to perform an action explicitly requested or authorized by the user.

For example, when a user authorizes LinkedIn posting, relevant content and authentication information may be transmitted to LinkedIn through LinkedIn's official APIs.

Booz may disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

8. Third-Party Services

Booz may integrate with third-party services such as LinkedIn and Telegram.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Booz does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

Users should review the privacy policies of services they choose to connect to Booz.

9. User Control

Users remain in control of the third-party accounts they connect to Booz.

  • Stop using Booz at any time
  • Revoke LinkedIn authorization
  • Disconnect connected services
  • Delete stored application credentials
  • Request deletion of information associated with Booz
